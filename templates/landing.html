1. Activation function 08:21

Definition: A function applied to a neuron's weighted sum that squashes the result into a fixed range, such as 0 to 1.

Explanation: Without it, stacked layers collapse into one linear map. The non-linearity is what lets a network learn curved decision boundaries.

Example: A sigmoid turns a sum of 4.2 into roughly 0.99, so the neuron is almost fully active.
